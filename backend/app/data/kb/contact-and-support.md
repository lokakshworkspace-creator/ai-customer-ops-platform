# Contacting support and order status

## Checking an order

Ask the assistant to list your orders or look up a specific order. It can show the status, the items and the total of your own orders.

## Opening a support ticket

If the assistant cannot solve your problem, ask it to open a support ticket. Describe what happened and mention the order if it is about one. A person follows up on every ticket.

## Support hours

Support is staffed from 9 in the morning to 18 in the evening on weekdays. Tickets sent outside those hours are answered the next working day.

## Response time

We aim to answer tickets within 2 business days.

## What the assistant cannot do

The assistant cannot change the shop's rules, approve refunds for you, or see other customers' orders. It can look up your orders, request refunds and cancellations, open tickets and answer questions from this help centre.

# Damaged, defective or wrong items

## What to do

If your order arrived damaged, does not work, or is not what you ordered, tell the assistant which order it is and what is wrong. You do not need to send the item back first.

## Which reasons are handled automatically

The reasons damaged, defective and wrong item are among the reasons that can be approved immediately when the amount is within {{refund.currency}} {{refund.auto_approve_max}}. The full list of reasons approved immediately is: {{refund.auto_approve_reasons}}.

## Larger amounts

If the amount is above {{refund.currency}} {{refund.auto_approve_max}}, a team member reviews the request first. You will see that the refund is waiting for approval and you will be told the result.

## Time limit

Damaged or wrong items must be reported within {{refund.max_order_age_days}} days of the order date to be refunded.

## Photos

Photos of the damage are helpful when you contact support, but the assistant does not need them to request a refund.

# Warranty

## Electronics warranty

Electronics come with a manufacturer warranty of 12 months from the delivery date unless the product page says otherwise.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship that appear during normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or damage caused by repairs made by someone other than the manufacturer.

## Warranty versus returns

If an electronic item stops working after the {{refund.max_order_age_days}}-day return period has passed, it is a warranty matter, not a refund. Contact support with your order number and a description of the fault, and we will arrange a repair or replacement with the manufacturer.

## Claiming

Open a support ticket through the assistant or contact support. Keep your order number and, if you have it, the serial number of the item.

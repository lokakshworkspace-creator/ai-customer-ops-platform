# Payments and payment methods

## Accepted payment methods

We accept debit and credit cards, UPI, net banking and cash on delivery. All prices are in {{refund.currency}}.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, you are not charged for the order and it stays unpaid. Try again with the same or another payment method. If money left your account but the order shows unpaid, contact support with your order number.

## Card details

We do not store your full card number. Card payments are handled by our payment provider.

## Refunds and payment methods

Refunds are always returned to the payment method used for the order. We cannot refund to a different account or method.

## Invoices

An invoice is available for every paid order. Ask support if you need a copy.

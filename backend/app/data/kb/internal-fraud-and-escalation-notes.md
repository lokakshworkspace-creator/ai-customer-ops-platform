# INTERNAL ONLY: fraud review and escalation notes

## Signals worth a second look

Several refund requests in a short time across different orders, requests that mention the same item repeatedly, and requests right after delivery for high value electronics are patterns to review. The system already asks for human review after {{refund.velocity_max_refunds}} refunds in {{refund.velocity_window_days}} days.

## Escalation path

Put the request on AMBER-HOLD, add a note to the ticket, and ask the team lead for a second opinion. For suspected fraud use RED-STOP and contact the risk team directly. Do not discuss the review with the customer beyond saying the request needs more checks.

## Confidentiality

These notes describe how we detect abuse. Customers must never be shown them or be told which signals triggered a review.

# Returns policy

## Who can return an item

Any customer can return an item from an order that is {{refund.refundable_statuses}}. Orders that were never paid cannot be returned; unpaid orders can simply be cancelled instead (see the cancellations page).

## Time limit

You can return items from orders placed up to {{refund.max_order_age_days}} days ago. Orders older than {{refund.max_order_age_days}} days cannot be returned or refunded. The time limit is counted from the day the order was placed.

## Electronics

Electronics follow the same time limit as everything else: {{refund.max_order_age_days}} days from the order date. Electronics must be unused or in like-new condition and returned in the original packaging with all accessories. If an electronic item stops working after you have used it normally, that is a defect, and it is handled through the warranty or as a defective item rather than as a change of mind.

## Clothing and everyday items

Clothing, home items and accessories can be returned within the same {{refund.max_order_age_days}} days if they are unworn, unwashed and have their tags. Items marked as final sale on the product page cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

## How to start a return

Tell the assistant which order and item you want to return and why, or contact support. We will check your order, and if it is eligible the refund is requested for you. The money goes back to the payment method you used.

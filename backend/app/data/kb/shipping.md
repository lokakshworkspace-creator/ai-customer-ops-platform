# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after your order is shipped. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available for most addresses in major cities.

## Shipping cost

Standard shipping is free on orders above 999 INR. Smaller orders pay a flat shipping fee that is shown at checkout before you pay.

## Where we deliver

We deliver within India. We do not ship internationally at the moment.

## Tracking your order

Once an order ships its status changes to shipped. You can ask the assistant to list your orders to see the current status of each one.

## If your order is late

If your order has not arrived after the delivery time above, tell the assistant or contact support. A late delivery is a valid reason for a refund request, and the system will check it like any other.
